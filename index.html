How to Play HD HOUSIE

1. Starting the Game:
Log in and either Create a Room (you become the Host) or Join a Room using a 5-letter code from your Host.


2. Getting Tickets:
Select 1 to 4 tickets. Set a custom bet amount per ticket. (If you have 0 coins, bets are waived and tickets are free!) Click Buy Tickets.


3. Drawing Numbers (Host Only):
The Host clicks Draw Next Number. The number appears on everyone's screen instantly.


4. Marking Tickets:
If the called number is on your ticket, simply tap/click it to stamp it.


5. Winning & Claiming:
- Early 5: Stamp any 5 numbers across all your tickets.
- 4 Corners: Stamp the first and last numbers on the top and bottom rows of a single ticket.
- Rows: Stamp all numbers in the Top, Middle, or Bottom row of a single ticket.
- Full House: Stamp all 15 numbers on a single ticket.
Click the corresponding Claim Prize button. The game will automatically verify your tickets and pay you!
